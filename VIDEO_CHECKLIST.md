# Tutorial Video Checklist

Videos to record for the Training section and YouTube channel. Ordered by priority: record top to bottom. Aim for one video per week minimum so the channel and training page grow steadily.

General tips for every video:
- Keep tutorials short. 2 to 5 minutes each beats one 30 minute monster. People search for one specific answer.
- Say the searchable phrase out loud in the first 10 seconds ("how to add a timestamp to surveillance video") because YouTube transcribes and indexes it.
- Use real-looking surveillance footage, not vacation clips. Your audience should see their own workflow.
- Title format: "How to X in Investigation Flow" for tutorials. Keep it literal, not clever.
- End every video pointing to the free demo or download page.

## Phase 1: The money video (record first)

- [ ] **The 60-90 second problem/solution video for the homepage hero.**
      Open on the pain: "Your client needs court-ready video with accurate timestamps, and your camera got the time wrong." Show the fix in real time: drop in a clip, correct the timestamp, export. No feature tour, just problem to solved in under 90 seconds. This one is marketing, not training, and it works on the homepage, YouTube pre-roll, and social.

## Phase 2: Getting started (the basics everyone needs)

- [ ] **Installing Investigation Flow on Windows** (2 min)
      Download, install, activate license. Show the download page briefly.
- [ ] **Installing Investigation Flow on Mac** (2 min)
      Same as above, including the macOS security prompt if there is one.
- [ ] **Your first project: add footage, timestamp it, export** (4-5 min)
      The complete happy path start to finish. This becomes the "Start Here" video on the training page.
- [ ] **How to upload footage into Investigation Flow** (2-3 min)
      Drag and drop, file picker, adding multiple files at once, what formats are accepted (MP4, MOV, AVI, and the rest).

## Phase 3: Core features (one video per feature)

- [ ] **How to add a court-ready timestamp to surveillance video** (3 min)
      The flagship feature. Setting the correct date and time, how the overlay stays frame-accurate.
- [ ] **How to fix a wrong timestamp from your camera** (3 min)
      Camera clock was off by an hour, DST issues, correcting the offset. High search value because this is the exact pain that brings people in.
- [ ] **How to customize timestamp appearance and position** (3 min)
      Format, font size, corner placement, whatever options exist.
- [ ] **How to use the timeline** (4 min)
      Navigating, trimming clips, reordering, zooming in on a moment.
- [ ] **How to stitch multiple clips into one video** (3-4 min)
      Combining a full day of surveillance into one sequence in chronological order.
- [ ] **How to keep or remove audio** (2 min)
      When to strip audio for court, when to keep it, doing both from one project.
- [ ] **How to grab still shots from video** (2 min)
      Frame-accurate stills for reports and exhibits.
- [ ] **How to convert video formats** (2-3 min)
      Getting proprietary or odd formats into something a lawyer's laptop can play.
- [ ] **How to batch process an entire case** (4 min)
      Timestamping and converting a whole folder at once. This is a differentiator, so show the time saved explicitly.

## Phase 4: Workflow videos (how PIs actually work)

- [ ] **From SD card to court-ready exhibit: a full case walkthrough** (6-8 min)
      The longest video. One realistic case processed end to end. Great for the training page and for convincing on-the-fence buyers.
- [ ] **Preparing video evidence your client's attorney will accept** (4 min)
      Timestamps, stills, file naming, export settings. Positions you as the expert, not just the software vendor.
- [ ] **Processing a multi-camera surveillance session** (4 min)
      Multiple angles or cameras from one day, stitched and labeled.
